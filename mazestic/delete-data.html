Delete Your Data

MAZEstic: Maze Worlds, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 10 November 2026

MAZEstic: Maze Worlds ("MAZEstic" or "the Game") by CTN Devs does not use user accounts, and I do not keep a profile about you. Most of your data is on your device or in your own Google account, so you can delete it yourself. The steps below explain how.

1. Delete everything from the Game

  1. Open MAZEstic and go to Settings > Your Data.
  2. Tap Delete Save Game and confirm twice.

This deletes your progress on the device, your times in the global best times, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Google Play Games. Your "Remove Ads" purchase is not affected. If you want to keep your cloud save, sign out first in Settings > Cloud Save.

2. If you no longer have the Game

Email mazestic@ctndevs.com with the subject "MAZEstic data deletion request". To find your best times I need your player id, shown in Settings > Your Data. Without it I cannot tell which times are yours.

Your cloud save is in your Google account. You can delete it from the Google Play Games settings on your device or in your Google account, by deleting the data for MAZEstic or your Play Games profile.

3. Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open your device's Settings > Apps > MAZEstic.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear data, or simply uninstall the Game.

This deletes the data on your device immediately. It does not delete your best times on the server or your cloud save; use step 1 for those.

4. Delete a bug report you sent

Bug reports, your email address if you gave one, and any picture you attached are deleted automatically within 90 days. To have yours deleted sooner, email mazestic@ctndevs.com with the date you sent it.

5. Advertising, analytics, and crash data

Google AdMob, GameAnalytics, and Sentry use their own identifiers, not your name or email. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Security & privacy > Ads, depending on your device)
  • change your consent for personalized ads in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options (shown in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland)
  • uninstall the Game to stop all further collection

This data is deleted automatically by these services after a limited time.

6. What is kept

After you follow the steps above, I keep no personal data about you, except purchase records that accounting law requires. If you have questions or want help with a deletion request, contact me at mazestic@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy.
