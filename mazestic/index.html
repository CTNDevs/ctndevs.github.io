About MAZEstic

A maze you steer with your hands

You tilt the phone, the ball rolls, and the only thing standing between you and the exit is how steady your hands are. Prefer a thumb? Switch to the touch joystick in Settings.

Every level is a fixed maze, the same for every player, so each one has a shape worth learning and a time worth beating. Stars are scattered along the way, and the quick route is rarely the one that collects them all.

Each world adds a single new rule to the same simple idea, so the game keeps surprising you without getting complicated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Answers to the most common questions about MAZEstic. Can't find what you need? Email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com.

Is MAZEstic free? Are there in-app purchases?

Yes, it is free. The only purchase is Remove Ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play. Balls, floors, walls, and trails are unlocked with the stars you collect, never with money.

Are there ads?

Yes. A banner shows during levels, every tenth level asks for one ad to open, and you can choose to watch an ad to double the stars of a run or to try a style for a while. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options. Remove Ads takes away the banner and the level ads.

How do I control the ball?

By tilting your device. The game reads your phone's motion sensor, the same one that rotates your screen. You can adjust how sensitive it feels in Settings, or switch to a touch joystick.

Do I need an account?

No. On Android the game can sign in to Google Play Games to keep a copy of your progress in your Google account, and you can sign out in Settings. There is no account of mine to create.

How does the global best time work?

Each level keeps a single fastest time, shared by everyone who plays. When you finish a level, your time is sent to that table, and if it is the fastest it becomes the one shown to all players. A crown appears on the levels where the record is yours.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, on Android, through Google Play Games: your progress is saved in your Google account and comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall. Without Play Games it stays on the device you played on.

Does MAZEstic work offline?

Yes. Every level is on your device and the whole game is playable without a connection. A connection is only used for the global best times, cloud save, and ads.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

In the game, open Settings > About > Report a Bug, describe what happened, and attach a screenshot if you have one. You can also email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com.

How can I delete my data?

Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game erases your progress on the device, your times in the global best times, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games. If you no longer have the game, follow the steps on the Delete your data page.
