About MAZEstic

A maze you steer with your hands

There is no joystick and no buttons. You tilt the phone, the ball rolls, and the only thing standing between you and the exit is how steady your hands are.

Every maze is built by hand rather than generated at random, so each one has a shape worth learning. Stars are scattered along the way, and the quick route is rarely the one that collects them all.

Each world adds a single new rule to the same simple idea, so the game keeps surprising you without ever needing a tutorial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Answers to the most common questions about MAZEstic. Can't find what you need? Email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com.

Is MAZEstic free? Are there in-app purchases?

Yes, it is free, and there are no in-app purchases. During early access every ball, floor, wall, world, and difficulty is unlocked from the start.

Are there ads?

Not during early access. The early access build shows no ads at all. If ads are added later to keep the game free, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated before that happens.

How do I control the ball?

By tilting your device. The game reads your phone's motion sensor, the same one that rotates your screen. You can adjust how sensitive it feels in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, no account, and no profile. Your progress is saved on your device.

How does the global best time work?

Each level keeps a single fastest time, shared by everyone who plays. When you finish a level, your time is sent to that table, and if it is the fastest it becomes the one shown to all players. A crown appears on the levels where the record is yours.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Not yet. Your stars, unlocked levels, and personal best times are stored on the device you played on. Only the global best times are shared. Cross-device progress is something I am looking at.

Does MAZEstic work offline?

Yes. Every level is on your device and the whole game is playable without a connection. A connection is only used to send and fetch the global best times.

Why is it called early access?

Because the game is still growing. Levels, worlds, and features will be added, and some things will change along the way. Your feedback during this stage genuinely shapes what comes next.

How can I delete my data?

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. To have your entry removed from the global best times, email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com.
