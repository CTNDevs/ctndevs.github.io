Privacy Policy

MAZEstic: Maze Worlds, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game MAZEstic: Maze Worlds ("MAZEstic" or "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by CTN Devs, an independent developer based in Romania ("I", "me"). I am responsible for the personal data described here, except where another company decides how its own service uses data. You can contact me at any time at mazestic@ctndevs.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. On Android, the Game can keep a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games account.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • When you finish a level, your time is sent to the global best-times table with a random player id. No name or email address is sent.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and asks for your consent where the law requires it.
  • The Game uses GameAnalytics for usage statistics and Sentry for crash and bug reports.
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your data from the Game at any time: Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game stores on your device:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, collected stars, best times, and achievements
  • your star points and the balls, floors, walls, and trails you have unlocked or chosen
  • the replay of your best runs, used for the ghost ball
  • your settings, such as language, sound, graphics, and controls
  • whether you own "Remove Ads"
  • your random player id (see section 4) and a copy of the global best times

This data stays on your device until you delete it (section 14), clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.

4. Global Best Times

Each level has one fastest time, shared by everyone who plays. When you finish a level, the Game sends your time, the level, and a random player id to my server. The player id is created by the Game; it is not linked to your device, your account, or your name. It lets the Game show a crown on the records that are yours, and lets you delete your times. You can see it in Settings > Your Data.

Only the fastest time for each level is kept. To prevent abuse, the server briefly limits how often times can be sent; your IP address is not stored. The server runs on Cloudflare and the data is stored in the European Union.

5. Google Play Games and Cloud Save

On Android, the Game uses Google Play Games to save a copy of your progress in your Google account, so you can get it back after a reinstall or on another device. The Game uses only this save; it does not read your name, email address, or friends list.

You can stop the Game from using Play Games in Settings > Cloud Save > Sign Out. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is funded by ads from Google AdMob: a banner during levels, and ads you can choose to watch, for example to open every tenth level, double your stars, or try a style for a while.

Google and its partners may use your advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and the ads you see, in order to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options, and you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and I never receive your payment details. Google gives me the order records I need for refunds and accounting.

8. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

The Game uses GameAnalytics to understand how it is played, for example which levels are too hard and which features are used. GameAnalytics receives an identifier it creates for your installation, your device model, operating system, country, the Game's version, and gameplay events such as levels started and finished. I only look at totals across all players. GameAnalytics is operated by GameAnalytics A/S, Denmark: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice

9. Crash and Bug Reports (Sentry)

If the Game crashes, a report is sent to Sentry so I can fix the problem. It contains technical details about the error, the Game's version, and your device model and operating system. It does not contain screenshots or anything you typed.

If you send a report from Settings > About > Report a Bug, it contains what you wrote, your email address if you entered one, and a picture if you attached one. The picture is one you choose yourself; the Game cannot see your other photos. I use your email address only to reply to your report.

Reports are stored in Sentry's European region, in Germany: https://sentry.io/privacy/

10. Motion Sensor

The Game reads your device's motion sensor to move the ball when you tilt it. This happens only on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

11. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contract: to provide what you use or buy, such as the best times, cloud save, purchases, and deleting your data.
  • Consent: for personalized ads where the law requires it, and for bug reports you choose to send.
  • Legitimate interest: to improve the Game, fix crashes, prevent abuse, and show non-personalized ads.
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting records of purchases.

12. Service Providers and International Transfers

The Game relies on Cloudflare, Google, GameAnalytics, and Sentry. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the safeguards required by EU law. I do not sell your personal data and do not share it except with these services or where the law requires it.

13. Data Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud save: in your Google account until you delete it.
  • Global best times: while the record stands, or until you delete them.
  • Crash and bug reports: up to 90 days.
  • Advertising and analytics data: according to Google's and GameAnalytics' own policies.
  • Purchase records: as long as accounting law requires.
  • Emails you send me: until the matter is resolved, and no longer than 12 months.

14. Deleting Your Data

In the Game, open Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game. After you confirm, it deletes your progress on the device, your best times on the server, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games. Your "Remove Ads" purchase is not affected.

If you no longer have the Game, or for any other data, follow the steps on the Delete Your Data page or email mazestic@ctndevs.com.

15. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use these rights, email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Romania, this is ANSPDCP, www.dataprotection.ro.

16. Children

The Game is suitable for everyone (rated PEGI 3), but it is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

17. Data Security

Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit, and I take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

18. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, I will give notice on this page or in the Game.

19. Contact

Email: mazestic@ctndevs.com
