Privacy Policy

MAZEstic: Maze Worlds, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game MAZEstic: Maze Worlds ("MAZEstic" or "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by CTN Devs, the name under which Dumitru-Adrian Constantinescu, an independent developer, publishes games ("I", "me"), at Strada Bechetului 184, 207570 Teasc, Romania. I am responsible for the personal data described here, except where another company decides how its own service uses data. You can contact me at any time at mazestic@ctndevs.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. On Android, the Game can keep a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games account.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • When you finish a level, your time is sent to the global best-times table with a random player id. No name or email address is sent.
  • Your time on the daily maze is sent to that day's leaderboard with the same player id and a made-up name, and deleted after 14 days.
  • In online races you play under a made-up name. The other players in your race can see your IP address, and nothing about a race is kept after it ends.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and asks for your consent where the law requires it.
  • The Game uses GameAnalytics for usage statistics (in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, only if you agree) and Sentry for crash and bug reports.
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your data from the Game at any time: Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game stores on your device:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, collected stars, best times, and achievements
  • your star points and the balls, floors, walls, and trails you have unlocked or chosen
  • the replay of your best runs, used for the ghost ball
  • your settings, such as language, sound, graphics, and controls
  • whether you own "Remove Ads"
  • your random player id (see section 4) and a copy of the global best times
  • your online race record: the generated name, race stars, rating, and how often you reached each place
  • your daily maze record: your best time on each of the last 30 days, your streak, and your reminder choice

This data stays on your device until you delete it (section 16), clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.

4. Global Best Times and the Daily Maze

Each level has one fastest time, shared by everyone who plays. When you finish a level, the Game sends your time, the level, and a random player id to my server. The player id is created by the Game; it is not linked to your device, your account, or your name. It lets the Game show a crown on the records that are yours, and lets you delete your times. Because the id is all that links the times to you, I cannot tell which times are yours without it, and I do not collect anything more to find out (Article 11 of the GDPR). You can see it in Settings > Your Data.

Only the fastest time for each level is kept. To prevent abuse, the server briefly limits how often times can be sent; your IP address is not stored. The server runs on Cloudflare and the data is stored in the European Union.

The daily maze has a leaderboard of its own for each day. When you finish it, the Game sends your time, the day, the same random player id, and the made-up name the Game gave you (the one used in online races, such as "Swift Comet"). Other players see your made-up name and your time in that day's top ten, and you see your own place. Only your best time of the day is kept, and each day's times are deleted from the server after 14 days.

5. Online Races

Online races let up to five players race on the same maze. You do not need an account, and you cannot type a name or send messages: the Game gives you a made-up name, such as "Swift Comet".

What the other players see: your made-up name, the ball you use, whether you are ready, your ball moving through the maze during the race, and your result.

Your IP address: so the race runs smoothly, your phone connects directly to the phones of the other players in your race, and they can see your IP address, as in most online games. When a direct connection is not possible, the race goes through Cloudflare, my server provider. Once your phone is connected to theirs, I cannot control what the other players do with your IP address; if you do not want them to see it, do not join online races. The Game tells you this before your first online race.

What is kept: nothing. My server only knows about a race while it is open, and forgets it when it ends. Your race stars and race statistics stay on your device.

6. Google Play Games and Cloud Save

On Android, the Game uses Google Play Games to save a copy of your progress in your Google account, so you can get it back after a reinstall or on another device. The Game uses only this save; it does not read your name, email address, or friends list.

You can stop the Game from using Play Games in Settings > Cloud Save > Sign Out. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is funded by ads from Google AdMob: a banner during levels, and ads you can choose to watch, for example to open every tenth level, double your stars, or try a style for a while.

Google and its partners may use your advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and the ads you see, in order to show and measure ads. Besides the banner and the ads you choose to watch, a short ad plays before every fifth online race you join or create. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options, and you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and I never receive your payment details. Google gives me the order records I need for refunds and accounting.

9. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

The Game uses GameAnalytics to understand how it is played, for example which levels are too hard and which features are used. GameAnalytics receives an identifier it creates for your installation, your device model, operating system, country, the Game's version, and gameplay events such as levels started and finished. I only look at totals across all players. GameAnalytics is operated by GameAnalytics A/S, Denmark: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, GameAnalytics is used only if you agree to it in the consent message the Game shows when it starts, the same one used for ads. If you do not agree, the Game sends nothing to GameAnalytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options. In that message, the statistics are covered by the purposes I use myself: "Store and/or access information on a device", "Measure content performance", "Understand audiences through statistics or combinations of data from different sources", and "Develop and improve services". Elsewhere, it is used to improve the Game, and you can object to it by emailing me.

10. Crash and Bug Reports (Sentry)

If the Game crashes or stops responding, a report is sent to Sentry so I can fix the problem. It contains technical details about the error: the Game's own recent log messages, which of its screens were open, the Game's version, and your device model and operating system. It does not contain screenshots or anything you typed.

If you send a report from Settings > About > Report a Bug, it contains what you wrote, your email address if you entered one, and a picture if you attached one. The picture is one you choose yourself; the Game cannot see your other photos. I use your email address only to reply to your report.

Crash reports are sent automatically, so I can find and fix problems and keep the Game working. Sentry processes them on my behalf, under its data processing terms, and they contain only the technical details described above. This is based on my legitimate interest in a game that works; it does not depend on the consent message. If you do not want crash reports sent, turn them off at any time in Settings > Your Data > Crash Reports (the Game asks you to close and reopen it, and the change takes effect from then); uninstalling the Game also stops them. A bug report you choose to send still goes. You can also object by emailing me, and I will delete the reports linked to your device.

Reports are stored in Sentry's European region, in Germany: https://sentry.io/privacy/

11. Motion Sensor and Reminders

The Game reads your device's motion sensor to move the ball when you tilt it. This happens only on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

On Android you can turn on a daily reminder that a new daily maze is ready (Settings > Notifications). It is off until you agree to it. The reminder is scheduled by your device for itself: no notification service or server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

12. Sharing and Ratings

You can share a picture of your results on Facebook or Instagram. The picture and its text go only to the app you choose; I do not receive anything.

The Game may ask you once to rate it on Google Play. Your rating goes to Google; the Game is not told what you gave.

13. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contract: to provide what you use or buy, such as the best times, online races, cloud save, purchases, and deleting your data.
  • Consent: for personalized ads and, in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, for usage statistics (GameAnalytics), where the law requires it, and for bug reports you choose to send.
  • Legitimate interest: to fix crashes, prevent abuse, show non-personalized ads, and, outside the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, improve the Game with usage statistics.
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting records of purchases.

14. Service Providers and International Transfers

The Game relies on Cloudflare, Google, GameAnalytics, and Sentry. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are protected by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework), where the provider is certified under it, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum). GameAnalytics and Sentry process data on my behalf, under their data processing terms. I do not sell your personal data and do not share it except with these services or where the law requires it.

15. Data Retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud save: in your Google account until you delete it.
  • Global best times: while the record stands, or until you delete them.
  • Daily maze times: 14 days, or until you delete them.
  • Online races: only while the race is open; nothing is kept afterwards.
  • Crash and bug reports: for as long as Sentry keeps them under its retention rules (see Sentry's privacy policy), currently up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google for the periods described in how Google retains data.
  • Usage statistics: pseudonymous data linked to GameAnalytics' own installation identifier, kept under its data retention and deletion policy, and deleted when the Game stops using the service; I only ever look at totals.
  • Purchase records: as long as accounting law requires.
  • Emails you send me: until the matter is resolved, and no longer than 12 months.

16. Deleting Your Data

In the Game, open Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game. After you confirm, it deletes your progress on the device, including your online race record, your best times and daily maze times on the server, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games. Your "Remove Ads" purchase is not affected.

If you no longer have the Game, or for any other data, follow the steps on the Delete Your Data page or email mazestic@ctndevs.com.

17. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use these rights, email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Romania, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or the authority of the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the law was broken; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

United States residents. I do not sell your personal information. Under some US state laws, such as California's (CCPA/CPRA), showing personalized ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and information about the ads you see, can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To opt out, delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings. You can also email me at mazestic@ctndevs.com to ask what information I hold about you, or to have it deleted. I will not treat you differently for using these rights.

18. Children

The Game is rated PEGI 3, so its content suits everyone, but it is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age at which a child can consent to online services on their own where they live (16 in Romania and several other countries in the European Economic Area). Younger players should ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to personalized ads or usage statistics. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

19. Data Security

Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit, and I take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

20. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, I will give notice on this page or in the Game.
