About Skyline Spark

A fireworks show that never stops

Start with a handful of sparklers over a quiet town. Before long the whole sky is yours: rockets, wheels, drone shows, lasers and supernovas over the Eiffel Tower, the pyramids and the Opera House.

Every firework you buy earns applause, every manager you hire keeps the show running while you are away, and every milestone changes how your fireworks look.

It is an idle game made to relax with: a few taps now and then, and a sky that keeps getting brighter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Answers to the most common questions about Skyline Spark. Can't find what you need? Email me at skylinespark@ctndevs.com.

Is Skyline Spark free? Are there in-app purchases?

Yes, it is free. The only purchase is Remove Ads, a one-time purchase through Google Play. Fireworks, cities and collection pieces are earned by playing, never with money.

Are there ads?

Yes. A banner shows at the bottom of the screen, and you can choose to watch an ad to double the applause you earned while away or to get a boost. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options. Remove Ads takes away the banner.

Does the game earn while I am not playing?

Yes. Every firework with a manager keeps launching while the game is closed, and the applause is waiting for you when you come back.

Do I need an account?

No. On Android the game can sign in to Google Play Games to keep a copy of your progress in your Google account, and you can sign out in Settings. There is no account of mine to create.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, on Android, through Google Play Games: your progress is saved in your Google account and comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall. Without Play Games it stays on the device you played on.

Does Skyline Spark work offline?

Yes. The whole game is on your device. A connection is only used for cloud save and ads.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

In the game, open Settings > About > Report a Bug, describe what happened, and attach a screenshot if you have one. You can also email me at skylinespark@ctndevs.com.

How can I delete my data?

Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game erases your progress on the device, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games. If you no longer have the game, follow the steps on the Delete your data page.
