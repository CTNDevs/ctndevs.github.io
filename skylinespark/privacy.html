Privacy Policy

Skyline Spark: Firework Tycoon, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Skyline Spark: Firework Tycoon ("Skyline Spark" or "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by CTN Devs, the name under which Dumitru-Adrian Constantinescu, an independent developer, publishes games ("I", "me"), at Strada Bechetului 184, 207570 Teasc, Romania. I am responsible for the personal data described here, except where another company decides how its own service uses data. You can contact me at any time at skylinespark@ctndevs.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. On Android, the Game can keep a copy of your progress in your Google Play Games account.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device. The Game has no server of its own and sends no progress, name or email address to me.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and asks for your consent where the law requires it.
  • The Game uses GameAnalytics for usage statistics (in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, only if you agree) and Sentry for crash and bug reports.
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your data from the Game at any time: Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game stores on your device:

  • your progress in each city: prestige, firework levels, managers, upgrades and milestones reached
  • the cities you have opened and the city you are in
  • the pieces of your collection (skies, landscape and colours) you have unlocked and the ones you wear
  • your achievements, and the prestige and boosts waiting for you when you come back
  • your settings, such as language, sound, graphics and notifications
  • whether you own "Remove Ads"
  • a random player id, created by the Game and shown in Settings > Your Data, which you can give me if you write to me about your game

This data stays on your device until you delete it (section 13), clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.

4. Google Play Games and Cloud Save

On Android, the Game uses Google Play Games to save a copy of your progress in your Google account, so you can get it back after a reinstall or on another device, and to show your achievements in Play Games. The Game uses only this save and your achievements; it does not read your name, email address, or friends list.

You can stop the Game from using Play Games in Settings > Cloud Save. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is funded by ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and ads you can choose to watch to double the prestige you earned while away or to get a boost.

Google and its partners may use your advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and the ads you see, in order to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options, and you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove Ads" is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play: it removes the banner and gives the ad rewards without an ad. Google handles the payment, and I never receive your payment details. Google gives me the order records I need for refunds and accounting.

7. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

The Game uses GameAnalytics to understand how it is played, for example which fireworks and cities players reach and which features are used. GameAnalytics receives an identifier it creates for your installation, your device model, operating system, country, the Game's version, and gameplay events. I only look at totals across all players. GameAnalytics is operated by GameAnalytics A/S, Denmark: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, GameAnalytics is used only if you agree to it in the consent message the Game shows when it starts, the same one used for ads. If you do not agree, the Game sends nothing to GameAnalytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options. In that message, the statistics are covered by the purposes I use myself: "Store and/or access information on a device", "Measure content performance", "Understand audiences through statistics or combinations of data from different sources", and "Develop and improve services". Elsewhere, it is used to improve the Game, and you can object to it by emailing me.

8. Crash and Bug Reports (Sentry)

If the Game crashes or stops responding, a report is sent to Sentry so I can fix the problem. It contains technical details about the error: the Game's own recent log messages, which of its screens were open, the Game's version, and your device model and operating system. It does not contain screenshots or anything you typed.

If you send a report from Settings > About > Report a Bug, it contains what you wrote, your email address if you entered one, and a picture if you attached one. The picture is one you choose yourself; the Game cannot see your other photos. I use your email address only to reply to your report.

Crash reports are sent automatically, so I can find and fix problems and keep the Game working. Sentry processes them on my behalf, under its data processing terms, and they contain only the technical details described above. This is based on my legitimate interest in a game that works; it does not depend on the consent message. If you do not want crash reports sent, turn them off at any time in Settings > Your Data > Crash Reports (the Game asks you to close and reopen it, and the change takes effect from then); uninstalling the Game also stops them. A bug report you choose to send still goes. You can also object by emailing me, and I will delete the reports linked to your device.

Reports are stored in Sentry's European region, in Germany: https://sentry.io/privacy/

9. Reminders

On Android you can turn on a daily reminder to collect the prestige your show earned (Settings > Notifications). It is off until you agree to it. The reminder is scheduled by your device for itself: no notification service or server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

10. Sharing and Ratings

You can share a picture of your show through your device's share menu. The picture and its text go only to the app you choose; I do not receive anything.

The Game may ask you once to rate it on Google Play. Your rating goes to Google; the Game is not told what you gave.

11. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contract: to provide what you use or buy, such as cloud save, purchases, and deleting your data.
  • Consent: for personalized ads and, in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, for usage statistics (GameAnalytics), where the law requires it, and for bug reports you choose to send.
  • Legitimate interest: to fix crashes, show non-personalized ads, and, outside the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, improve the Game with usage statistics.
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting records of purchases.

12. Service Providers and International Transfers

The Game relies on Google, GameAnalytics, and Sentry. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are protected by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework), where the provider is certified under it, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum). GameAnalytics and Sentry process data on my behalf, under their data processing terms. I do not sell your personal data and do not share it except with these services or where the law requires it.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud save: in your Google account until you delete it.
  • Crash and bug reports: for as long as Sentry keeps them under its retention rules (see Sentry's privacy policy), currently up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google for the periods described in how Google retains data.
  • Usage statistics: pseudonymous data linked to GameAnalytics' own installation identifier, kept under its data retention and deletion policy, and deleted when the Game stops using the service; I only ever look at totals.
  • Purchase records: as long as accounting law requires.
  • Emails you send me: until the matter is resolved, and no longer than 12 months.

In the Game, open Settings > Your Data > Delete Save Game. After you confirm, it deletes your progress on the device and your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games. Your "Remove Ads" purchase is not affected. If you no longer have the Game, follow the steps on the Delete Your Data page or email skylinespark@ctndevs.com.

14. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To use these rights, email me at skylinespark@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Romania, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or the authority of the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the law was broken; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

United States residents. I do not sell your personal information. Under some US state laws, such as California's (CCPA/CPRA), showing personalized ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and information about the ads you see, can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To opt out, delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings. You can also email me at skylinespark@ctndevs.com to ask what information I hold about you, or to have it deleted. I will not treat you differently for using these rights.

15. Children

The Game is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age at which a child can consent to online services on their own where they live (16 in Romania and several other countries in the European Economic Area). Younger players should ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to personalized ads or usage statistics. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

16. Data Security

Data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit, and I take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

17. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, I will give notice on this page or in the Game.
