Delete Your Data

Skyline Spark: Firework Tycoon, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Skyline Spark: Firework Tycoon ("Skyline Spark" or "the Game") by CTN Devs does not use user accounts and has no server of its own, and I do not keep a profile about you. Your data is on your device or in your own Google account, so you can delete it yourself. The steps below explain how.

1. Delete everything from the Game

  1. Open Skyline Spark and go to Settings > Your Data.
  2. Tap Delete Save Game and confirm.

This deletes your progress on the device, and your cloud save if you are signed in to Google Play Games. Your "Remove Ads" purchase is not affected. If you want to keep your cloud save, sign out first in Settings > Cloud Save.

2. If you no longer have the Game

Your cloud save is in your Google account. You can delete it from the Google Play Games settings on your device or in your Google account, by deleting the data for Skyline Spark or your Play Games profile. For anything else, email skylinespark@ctndevs.com with the subject "Skyline Spark data deletion request".

3. Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open your device's Settings > Apps > Skyline Spark.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear data, or simply uninstall the Game.

This deletes the data on your device immediately. It does not delete your cloud save; use step 1 or step 2 for that.

4. Delete a bug report you sent

Bug reports, your email address if you gave one, and any picture you attached are deleted automatically when Sentry's retention period ends, currently within 90 days. To have yours deleted sooner, email skylinespark@ctndevs.com with the date you sent it.

5. Advertising, analytics, and crash data

Google AdMob, GameAnalytics, and Sentry use their own identifiers, not your name or email. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Security & privacy > Ads, depending on your device)
  • change your consent for personalized ads and usage statistics in Settings > Legal > Privacy Options (shown in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland)
  • turn off crash reports in Settings > Your Data > Crash Reports, then close and reopen the Game for the change to take effect
  • uninstall the Game to stop all further collection

This data is deleted automatically by these services after a limited time.

6. What is kept

After you follow the steps above, I keep no personal data about you, except purchase records that accounting law requires. If you have questions or want help with a deletion request, contact me at skylinespark@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy.
