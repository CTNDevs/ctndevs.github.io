Delete Your Data

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 25 September 2026

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle ("PuzzleMe") by CTN Devs does not use user accounts, and I do not keep a profile about you. Most of your data is stored on your device or in your own Google account, so you can delete it yourself. The steps below explain how to delete each type of data.

1. Delete your cloud saves

Cloud saves (progress, player name, and avatar) are stored in your Google Play Games account.

  1. Open PuzzleMe and go to the cloud save screen.
  2. Open the list of your saves and delete the ones you want to remove. They are deleted from Google's servers immediately.
  3. Optionally, turn off automatic cloud saving so no new saves are created.

You can also delete all PuzzleMe data from your Google Play Games account in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

2. Delete data stored on your device

Progress, statistics, settings, your player name, your avatar, and cached puzzle images are stored on your device.

  1. Open your device's Settings → Apps → PuzzleMe.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear data, or simply uninstall the Game.

This data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered, unless you have a cloud save.

3. Delete feedback you sent

Feedback messages, and your email address if you provided one, are deleted automatically as soon as I have read them, and no later than 7 days after they are received. To have them deleted sooner, email puzzleme@ctndevs.com with the subject "PuzzleMe data deletion request".

4. Advertising, analytics, and crash data

Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics process pseudonymous data linked to device identifiers, not to your name or email. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Security & privacy → Ads, depending on your device)
  • withdraw consent for personalized ads from the privacy options in the Game's settings
  • uninstall the Game to stop all further collection

This data is deleted automatically by Google after a limited retention period: analytics data within 14 months at most, and crash reports within 90 days.

5. What is kept

After you follow the steps above, I keep no personal data about you. If you have any questions, or want help with a deletion request, contact me at puzzleme@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy.
