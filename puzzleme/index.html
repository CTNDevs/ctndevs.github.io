Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Answers to the most common questions about PuzzleMe.

How many difficulty levels are available?

Eight, from beginner-friendly 3×3 puzzles to the challenging 16×16 mode.

Can I create puzzles from my own photos?

Yes. Choose a picture from your gallery and PuzzleMe turns it into a custom jigsaw puzzle.

Does PuzzleMe work offline?

Yes. Most puzzles can be played without an internet connection, so it's perfect for travel or relaxing anywhere.

Will my progress be saved?

Absolutely. Your current puzzle is saved automatically, so you can continue exactly where you left off. You can also use cloud save with Google Play Games to continue on another device, and view or delete your saves anytime.

Do I need to create an account?

No. PuzzleMe does not require an account to play.

Are my personal photos uploaded?

No. Photos used for custom puzzles always stay on your device and are never uploaded.
