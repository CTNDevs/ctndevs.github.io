About PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle

A relaxing puzzle experience for everyone

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle combines beautiful artwork, smooth gameplay, and relaxing music to create a jigsaw puzzle experience you can enjoy anytime. Whether you have a few minutes or a few hours, there's always a puzzle waiting for you.

Explore thousands of carefully selected images across dozens of categories, choose from eight difficulty levels ranging from beginner-friendly 3×3 puzzles to challenging 16×16 grids, or create your own custom puzzles from photos stored on your device.

Every puzzle is designed to be satisfying to solve, with helpful tools like hints, edge-piece filtering, full image preview, automatic saving, and customizable backgrounds to make every session enjoyable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Answers to the most common questions about PuzzleMe.

How many difficulty levels are available?

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle offers eight difficulty levels ranging from beginner-friendly 3×3 puzzles to the challenging 16×16 mode.

Can I create puzzles from my own photos?

Yes. Simply choose a picture from your gallery and PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle will transform it into a custom jigsaw puzzle.

Does PuzzleMe work offline?

Yes. Most puzzles can be enjoyed without an internet connection, making PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle perfect for travel or relaxing anywhere.

Will my progress be saved?

Absolutely. Your current puzzle is automatically saved so you can continue exactly where you left off.

Do I need to create an account?

No. PuzzleMe does not require an account to play.

Are my personal photos uploaded?

No. Photos used for custom puzzles always remain on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
