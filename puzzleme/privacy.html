Privacy Policy

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle by CTN Devs

PuzzleMe

Developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 08 March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the game PuzzleMe handles information.

2. Personal Data

PuzzleMe does not require users to create accounts and does not automatically collect personal information.

3. Gameplay Data Stored on Device

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • player name
  • avatar image
  • puzzles started or completed
  • rewards earned
  • play time
  • settings

4. Cloud Save

Users may optionally enable cloud save through Google Play Games services.

When enabled, gameplay information may be synchronized with the user's Google Play Games account. These services are operated by Google.

5. Custom Images

Players may create puzzles using images stored on their own device. These images remain on the device and are not uploaded.

6. Game Images and Firebase

Puzzle images are hosted using Google Firebase Storage. Firebase is also used to manage references to images required for the Game.

7. Feedback

The Game includes a feedback feature. When users send feedback, the following may be stored temporarily:

  • feedback message
  • email address (optional)

Feedback messages and email addresses are stored using Firebase Firestore and deleted after review.

8. Advertising

PuzzleMe displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may use device identifiers or advertising identifiers to display ads. Google's privacy policy applies to the use of these identifiers.

For more information about how Google uses data in advertising, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

9. Analytics

PuzzleMe may use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous technical data.

10. GDPR Notice

PuzzleMe is developed in Romania and is subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The Game does not collect personal data directly. Data stored locally on your device (player name, avatar, game progress) is under your own control and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the Game.

Feedback messages submitted voluntarily are the only personal data that may be processed. These are deleted after review. You may request deletion of your feedback by contacting us at: puzzleme@ctndevs.com

For data processed by third-party services (Google AdMob, Firebase), please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

11. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

13. Contact

Email: puzzleme@ctndevs.com
