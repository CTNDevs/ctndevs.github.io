Privacy Policy

PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle, developed and published by CTN Devs

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game PuzzleMe - Jigsaw Puzzle ("PuzzleMe" or "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by CTN Devs, an independent developer based in Romania ("I", "me").

I am the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact me at any time at puzzleme@ctndevs.com.

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play.
  • Photos you use for custom puzzles never leave your device.
  • Your progress, player name, and avatar are stored on your device. Saving them to your Google Play Games account is optional and fully under your control.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services process device identifiers and technical data.
  • Feedback you send is deleted within 7 days.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • player name and avatar image
  • puzzles started or completed
  • rewards earned
  • play time and statistics
  • settings

This data stays under your control and is deleted when you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.

4. Cloud Save (Google Play Games)

Cloud save is optional. Using Google Play Games services, you can save your progress, player name, and avatar to your Google Play Games account, so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling.

From the Game you can:

  • save your progress manually with the save button
  • turn automatic cloud saving on or off
  • view your cloud saves and delete them from Google's servers

Cloud saves are stored by Google in your account and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. I do not have access to them.

5. Custom Puzzles and Avatar Images

You can create puzzles from photos stored on your device. These photos are read only on your device and are never uploaded by the Game.

If you choose an avatar image from your gallery, it is stored on your device and, if you use cloud save, saved with your progress in your Google Play Games account, as described in section 4. It is not sent to me.

6. Puzzle Images (Firebase Storage)

Puzzle images are hosted on Google Firebase Storage and downloaded to your device when needed. To deliver them, Google processes technical data such as your IP address. Downloaded images may be cached on your device to load faster.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner ad is shown during gameplay
  • full-screen rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one to receive a hint

Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and ad interactions to show ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the privacy options in the Game's settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings. More information about how Google uses data in advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

8. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used and to improve it. Firebase Analytics collects data such as an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, approximate location (country, derived from your IP address), and in-game events such as puzzles started or completed. This data is pseudonymous: it does not include your name or email address.

9. Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The Game uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports so I can find and fix problems. A crash report contains technical information such as a Crashlytics installation identifier, device model, operating system version, the state of the Game at the time of the crash, and the error details.

10. Feedback

The Game includes a feedback feature. When you send feedback, the following is stored using Google Firebase Firestore:

  • your feedback message
  • your email address, only if you choose to provide it

I use this information only to read your feedback and, if you left an email address, to reply to you. Feedback is deleted as soon as I have read it, and no later than 7 days after it is received.

11. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Consent for personalized advertising, where required by law. You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 7.
  • Legitimate interest in showing non-personalized ads to keep the Game free, in delivering game content, in understanding how the Game is used, and in fixing crashes.
  • Your request when you send feedback and ask for a reply.

12. Service Providers and International Transfers

The Game relies on services provided by Google (Google Play Games services, Google AdMob, Google Firebase Storage, Firestore, Analytics, and Crashlytics). Google may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are protected by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

I do not sell your personal data and do not share it with anyone else.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

13. Data Retention

  • Data on your device: until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.
  • Cloud saves in Google Play Games: until you delete them from the Game or from your Google account.
  • Feedback and email addresses: no longer than 7 days.
  • Analytics and crash data: for the limited periods set in Google Firebase, after which it is deleted automatically.

14. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interest
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before

Step-by-step instructions for deleting your data are available on the Delete Your Data page.

To exercise these rights, email me at puzzleme@ctndevs.com. I will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Romania, this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro.

15. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience (rated PEGI 3), but it is not specifically directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, for example through the feedback feature, please contact me and I will delete it.

16. Data Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, and I rely on Google's security for data processed by its services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

17. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, I will provide a notice on this page or in the Game.

18. Contact

Email: puzzleme@ctndevs.com
